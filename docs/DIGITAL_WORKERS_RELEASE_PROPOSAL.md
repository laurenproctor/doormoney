# Digital workers: project-page placement and release path

**Status:** Placement scope approved; evidence policy proposed. Migration 20260924172831 opens publication and Stripe test-mode purchases. The policy remains closed for live money.

## Inventory

Three placement templates exist: Monthly email signature, Virtual meeting background and Project page credit. The worker chooses the actual option, price, month, eligible account, calls or page, sponsor wording, expected appearances and reach basis in the offer editor. A starter kit is an editable example, not an offer. The research kit suggests the email signature only.

An eligible email is one the worker sends from an account they control and may use for sponsorship. An eligible meeting is one where the worker controls the background and may display the credit. An eligible project page is public, under the worker's control and carries an explicitly labeled sponsor credit during the agreed dates. Client-owned accounts or pages, private correspondence, confidential calls and participant identities do not become sponsor inventory through a template.

The project-page offer names the public URL, its placement near the project introduction or another specified section, the credit text, optional sponsor link, start and end dates, and what happens if the page moves or is unavailable. A sponsor does not control the project, product claims or research findings. The page credit is a placement, not a claim about visits or clicks. A page can be inspected directly while the credit is live; the worker supplies a dated capture to preserve the record.

## Proposed delivery and evidence

| Placement | Contract to record before publication | Proposed private evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly email signature | Sending account or eligible message scope, sponsor text or mark, start and end dates, planned sending basis | Dated redacted signature sample and count of eligible messages sent during the agreed month. A send count does not claim opens or views. |
| Virtual meeting background | Eligible meeting types, sponsor text or mark, start and end dates, planned meeting basis | Dated solo preview of the background and count of qualifying calls on which it was used. No attendee names, screenshots of participants or client information. |
| Project page credit | Public page URL, exact section, sponsor text or mark, optional link, start and end dates | Dated capture showing the labeled credit and the public URL. No claim about visits, clicks or conversion without separate evidence. |

Use the existing evidence release rule: accept the sponsor's materials first, then release each deliverable's share when its evidence is attached. An unmet deliverable stays held under the existing policy; the organizer cannot rewrite cancellation or refund behavior in the offer. The offer must state the quantity and delivery window that the worker can support. Any reach estimate must have its basis; neither template promises that a recipient read an email or a participant noticed a background.
