# Digital workers: proposed release policy

**Status:** Proposal for review. The category still saves private drafts only. This document does not open publication or payments.

## Inventory

Only two placement templates exist: Monthly email signature and Virtual meeting background. The worker chooses the actual option, price, month, eligible account or calls, sponsor wording, expected appearances and reach basis in the offer editor. A starter kit is an editable example, not an offer.

An eligible email is one the worker sends from an account they control and may use for sponsorship. An eligible meeting is one where the worker controls the background and may display the credit. Client-owned accounts, private correspondence, confidential calls and participant identities do not become sponsor inventory through the template.

## Proposed delivery and evidence

| Placement | Contract to record before publication | Proposed private evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly email signature | Sending account or eligible message scope, sponsor text or mark, start and end dates, planned sending basis | Dated redacted signature sample and count of eligible messages sent during the agreed month. A send count does not claim opens or views. |
| Virtual meeting background | Eligible meeting types, sponsor text or mark, start and end dates, planned meeting basis | Dated solo preview of the background and count of qualifying calls on which it was used. No attendee names, screenshots of participants or client information. |

Use the existing evidence release rule: accept the sponsor's materials first, then release each deliverable's share when its evidence is attached. An unmet deliverable stays held under the existing policy; the organizer cannot rewrite cancellation or refund behavior in the offer. The offer must state the quantity and delivery window that the worker can support. Any reach estimate must have its basis; neither template promises that a recipient read an email or a participant noticed a background.

## Steps before launch

1. Approve the eligible account and meeting scope, proposed evidence, and how a month of appearances becomes deliverables in an offer.
2. Add a Digital workers delivery policy in **proposed** status and run a purchase, materials approval, evidence release and refund in test mode.
3. Enable publishing in a separate change and remove the three kits' `draftOnly` flags once the public offer and review screens are checked with these placements.
4. Activate the policy for live payments only after those flows and sponsor-facing terms have been reviewed. Patron preference is already enabled and does not affect any of these gates.

No further placement template is part of this proposal.
