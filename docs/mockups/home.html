How it works

What sponsorship does for a band

For bands
1
Musicians list their surfaces

Kick head, case spots, straps, tip jar, posts. Musicians set the prices and keep the final say. Nothing goes up without their yes.

2
A patron backs the run

A local business or a gear brand takes a surface and puts the money up before the first show.

3
The money reaches the musician

Payment reaches the band as the run happens, every week. No invoices, no chasing, no waiting on a check.

For patrons
1
Pick a band

The Sunday band at the corner bar, a touring act coming through, or a whole neighborhood circuit of rooms.

2
Door Money holds the money

Door Money charges nothing until the placement actually runs, at a real show, in front of a real crowd. A placement that never goes up never costs a patron a cent.

3
Patrons see where it went

A record of the whole run: the shows the band played, the rooms, and how many people filled them. Support a patron can point at, not just spend.

House rules

The rules everyone plays by

Stage rules

  1. Nothing goes up without the band's yes.
  2. Patrons put the money up before the first show.
  3. Bands get paid every Friday. No chasing.
  4. Patrons pay nothing for a placement that never runs.
  5. No placements at weddings or private events.

The widget

On the band's own site too

Every act on Door Money gets a widget for their own website. Fans and local businesses back the run right there, on the page they already visit, and the money reaches the band on the same weekly schedule.
