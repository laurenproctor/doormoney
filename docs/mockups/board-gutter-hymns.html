If a bid wins

What happens after Friday

  1. The winning patron puts the money up within 48 hours, or the spot goes to the next bid.
  2. The band approves the mark. Nothing goes up without their yes.
  3. The band plays the run it was already playing.
  4. The money reaches the band week by week as the run goes on.
  5. End of the run, the patron gets a record of it: every show, every room, the attendance count.
